Poster QR codes

Paste the link for a poster to get a QR code in Uni Bonn blue with the BCDSS chain mark in the centre, ready for print.

Enter a full link starting with https:// or http://.

Centre mark

Use a square version of the mark (SVG or PNG), not the wide Uni Bonn lockup. A transparent or white background works best.

Style

Classic squares and Fluid are the most forgiving for small prints.

For posters read from about a metre away, aim for 10 cm or more.

This link makes a dense code. A shorter link (for example the Plone page without tracking parameters) will scan more reliably.
